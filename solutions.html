Two ways to work with us, at four sizes.

Hand us the vehicle side and receive the software, or license the platform and keep the engineering in your own team. Either way, you choose how much of the vehicle it covers.

Full-stack integration

SDVsolution takes the vehicle side end to end: requirements, generated software and ECU integration across the complete vehicle, delivered against your vehicle releases.

Tool license

Questions we are asked

Who can sign up?

Access is by invitation. Request a demo, and after the walkthrough we enable the email addresses of your team.

Do we need training?

No. If you have used a requirements tool, the tree, the document view and the import and export will be familiar. The assistant answers questions inside the app.

Does the AI replace our engineers?

No. Agents draft requirements and propose behavior models. An engineer reviews and approves each one, and only approved work goes into a baseline or a release package.

Can we keep using our current requirements tool?

Yes. Requirements can be imported and exported, and the Custom plan includes integrations with the tools your program already uses.

What do we receive when we license generated software?

Source code, tests and a build file, for a feature, an ECU or the complete vehicle. The software factory page lists the contents of a package.

Tell us the size of your program.

Name the feature, the ECU or the vehicle, and we will come back with a scope and a quote. We reply by email.
