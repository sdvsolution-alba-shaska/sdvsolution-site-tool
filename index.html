Product

One platform, the whole V.

From stakeholder needs to verified software and hardware — with the rigor auditors expect and the speed teams want.

✦

AI assistant

Drop a PDF, Excel, Word or DBC/LDF/ARXML file and ask in plain language. It reads the file, answers grounded in your live model, and drafts requirements and updates — which your engineers review and approve. The assistant supports your team; it doesn't replace engineering judgment.

▤

End-to-end V-model

System (SYS.2–5), software (SWE.1–6) and hardware (HWE.1–4) requirements with architecture, detailed design and state machines — one connected model.

✓

ISO 29148 quality gate

Every requirement checked live for EARS syntax, verifiability, singularity and unambiguity — quality built in, not bolted on.

Live traceability & impact

Click any requirement or signal and see everything it derives from and everything a change would affect — up and down the V.

Reviews & baselines

How it works

Get to a defensible baseline, fast.

1

Capture

Elicit stakeholder needs and structure them into system requirements using the guided EARS template.

2

Derive

Roll requirements down to software and hardware with architecture, detailed design and allocation to ECUs.

3

Verify

Attach measurable acceptance criteria and test cases; the quality gate keeps every requirement testable.

4

Review & baseline

Approve through the review board, freeze a baseline, and export the SyRS/SRS and comm matrix.
