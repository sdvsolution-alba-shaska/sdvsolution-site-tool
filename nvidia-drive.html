SDVsolution / NVIDIA DRIVE

The vehicle around NVIDIA DRIVE AGX Thor.

Choosing the central computer is one decision. Connecting it to the dozens of other ECUs in the vehicle is the larger job. SDVsolution models that vehicle for you, in two ready-made architectures.

Generation 1: driver assistance on Thor

The driver-assistance ECU is built on NVIDIA DRIVE AGX Thor. The cockpit keeps its own computer and the zone controllers stay as they are.

  • The lowest-change path onto NVIDIA DRIVE
  • Driver-assistance interfaces, cameras, radars and ultrasonic sensors modeled
  • Available as a ready-made project in SDVsolution

Generation 2: driver assistance and cockpit on one Thor

Both functions run on a single Thor-based central computer, with the zone controllers around it.

  • One central computer in place of two
  • Driver-assistance and cockpit interfaces on one ECU specification
  • Cockpit audio, displays and cameras brought onto the central computer's interfaces
  • Available as a ready-made project in SDVsolution

What we bring to a Thor-based program

Vehicle architectureThe topology around the central computer: zone controllers, sensors, actuators and power distribution.
Communication matrixCAN, LIN and Ethernet frames and signals for every ECU, with the interface requirements derived from them.
ECU specificationsA requirement specification for each ECU in the vehicle, ready to hand to its supplier.
Release trackingSoftware delivery, evidence and sign-off for every ECU against each vehicle release.

Does the hardware cover the vehicle?

Before you commit to an architecture, the platform compares what the vehicle needs from its central computer with the interfaces the hardware provides, line by line.

What is comparedWhy it matters
Camera and display linksWhether every camera and display in the vehicle has a port, in both generations
Ethernet, CAN and LINWhether the vehicle's buses can all reach the central computer, directly or through a zone controller
AudioWhether the cockpit's audio buses move onto the central computer in Generation 2
Supply and environmentWhether voltage range and temperature limits fit where the unit is mounted
